Knapsack Lab

Compare the greedy fractional knapsack with the 0/1 dynamic programming solution, and watch the DP table fill in cell by cell.

Capacity Presets:
Up to 8 items. Weights are whole numbers, capacity up to 50.

Results

Fractional knapsack (greedy by value/weight)

0/1 knapsack: dynamic programming table

Recurrence: dp[i][w] = max( dp[i-1][w], value[i] + dp[i-1][w-weight[i]] ). Time and space O(n·W).
